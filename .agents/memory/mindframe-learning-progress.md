---
name: Mindframe learning progress
description: Durable progress-state convention for Mindframe’s educational districts.
---

Educational districts should track their completed lessons separately from completed murder cases, while sharing the player’s XP total.

**Why:** Cases and lessons are different learning activities with different completion counts and unlock rules. Combining their IDs would make progress summaries ambiguous and could cause one activity to overwrite another.

**How to apply:** Give each future district its own completion collection and XP reward path. Keep the city desk responsible for presenting the combined player progress.
