**City Hub (overall intro):**
> Vantage City runs on lies. Not the loud kind — the quiet kind. The ones
> dressed up as facts, hidden in a suspect's story, buried in a headline
> nobody double-checks.
>
> Every citizen here has been fooled at least once. Most don't know it. The
> ones pulling the strings — the deceivers, the manipulators, the ones who
> profit from confusion — count on that.
>
> You're not most people.
>
> You're a detective. And tonight, you start pulling back the curtain on this
> city's dark side — one lie, one bias, one bad argument at a time.
>
> Vantage City doesn't give up its secrets easy. But it's about to meet
> someone who won't stop asking questions.
>
> Welcome to the city. Time to see it clearly.

**Crime Scene:**
> This is where the city hides its bodies — and its excuses. Every murder here
> comes with three suspects, three stories, and one truth nobody wants to say
> out loud. Your job isn't to guess who did it. It's to prove it.

**Bias District:**
> Nobody built this part of the city on purpose — it just grew, one shortcut
> at a time. The streets curve where people stopped asking questions. The
> buildings lean where assumptions held them up instead of facts. Walk
> carefully. Your own mind lives here too.

**Fallacy District:**
> Every con artist in Vantage City trained here first. This is where bad
> arguments learn to wear good clothes — where "everyone agrees" becomes
> proof, and "either this or that" becomes the only two doors in the room.
> Come see the tricks up close. You'll never fall for them the same way twice.

**Detective Academy:**
> Before you're any good on the street, you're good in the room. This is
> where the city's sharpest detectives learned to separate a fact from a
> feeling — and where you're about to do the same.

**Decision Lab:**
> Out here, there's no pausing to think. One choice, one consequence, no
> take-backs — just like real life. The Lab exists to show you exactly how
> good your instincts really are.

**Argument Analyzer:**
> Not every case in this city has a body. Some just have bad reasoning —
> dressed up, confident, and wrong. Bring your own. We'll help you take it
> apart.

**Mind Palace:**
> The city forgets fast. People relearn the same lies over and over because
> nothing sticks. This room is built to make sure it does — for you, at
> least.

**Achievements:**
> Every district you've cracked left a mark. This is where the city keeps
